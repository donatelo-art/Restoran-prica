# Photo intake

Drop the restaurant's real photographs in this folder. Anything here gets
processed into `public/images/` with the filenames the site already expects,
so no component code changes.

## What to send

Name files by slot if you can — otherwise just drop them in and say which is
which, and they'll be matched up.

The menu is set typographically rather than as photo cards — the way fine
dining menus are normally presented — so there are no per-dish slots. Food
photography belongs in the gallery instead.

| Slot | Shape | What it should show |
|---|---|---|
| `o-nama-terasa` | landscape 3:2 | The terrace / the Danube view — the hero of the About section |
| `o-nama-zar` | square | The grill, fire, someone cooking. Also appears beside the menu preview on the home page, so make it a strong one |
| `o-nama-vino` | square | Wine — Matalj, Deurić, Kovačević bottles or a poured glass |
| `galerija-obala` | landscape | The riverbank. This is the largest tile in the gallery |
| `galerija-ulov` | square | The morning catch, fresh fish |
| `galerija-zalazak` | square | Sunset over the water |
| `galerija-sto` | square | A laid table by the water |
| `galerija-kuhinja` | square | The kitchen at work |
| `galerija-vece` | landscape | Evening atmosphere, full-width band |

Highest resolution available is best — they get downscaled during processing,
and starting large keeps them sharp on retina screens.

## A note on sourcing

Photos from the restaurant's own Instagram and Facebook are fine to use here.

**TripAdvisor photos are usually not.** Most images on a TripAdvisor listing
are uploaded by guests, who keep the copyright to them. Reusing those on the
restaurant's own commercial website is a real risk, even though they appear
on the restaurant's page. Stick to photos the restaurant took or commissioned.

If a professional photographer shot any of these, check what the contract
says about web use — some licenses cover print or social only.
